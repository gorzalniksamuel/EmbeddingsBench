Did the MLP lack capacity or overfit?

An automatic search (Optuna TPE, 40 trials per dataset) tried depth 0–3, width 64–4,096, dropout, input dropout, weight decay and learning rate, each trial with early stopping on validation. Every dot is one trial: training accuracy (open) and validation accuracy (filled) against the number of trainable parameters. Both low = too little capacity (bias); training far above validation = overfitting (variance). The star marks the trial chosen on validation; depth 0 is a plain linear classifier.

When does a neural network pay off?

Test accuracy when the classifier sees only k labelled examples per class (k = 1 … 512, then the full training set; mean of 3 random draws for k below 32). LogReg and the MLP use their validation-selected settings, and the MLP stops early on the full validation split. Dashed: zero-shot (no labels). Dotted: TF-IDF trained on the full set.

Fine-tuning the encoder

Dataset

LoRA (rank 16, alpha 32, dropout 0.05) on every attention and MLP projection of the encoder, plus a linear head initialised from the frozen-embedding logistic regression; bf16 with gradient checkpointing, AdamW with linear warm-up and decay, cross-entropy on the training split, first 512 tokens. Validation accuracy is measured four times per epoch; the best checkpoint is the one scored on test. Step 0 is the untouched encoder with the LogReg head.

Do 2,048 tokens help over 512?

The same classifiers with the same settings, fed embeddings of the first 512 or the first 2,048 tokens of each text. Texts shorter than 512 tokens get identical embeddings, so any difference comes from the long texts.

How small can the embeddings be?

jina v5 embeddings are trained so that their first dimensions carry the most information (Matryoshka). Here LogReg (same C) uses only the first d of the 1,024 dimensions, re-normalised. Smaller vectors mean less storage and faster search.

Model registry

Every trained model is a numbered version under a name. Training again under an existing name creates the next version; "Retrain" starts from a version's settings. Each version keeps its split, settings, metrics, parameters, training effort, artifacts and your description. The same operations are available from the shell: python bench/jobs.py --help.

Splits

        
A split fixes which texts are used for training, validation and testing. Splits are versioned and never change once built; to try different sizes or another seed, create a new one and train models on it. Validation and test texts that duplicate a training text are always removed. Texts that are not embedded yet are embedded on the GPU when the split is built.

      

      

      

      

    

    

      
Add a dataset

        
Upload a CSV or JSONL file with the columns text and label, and optionally split (train, validation, test). Without a split column the rows are split 80/10/10, stratified by label. Duplicate texts and classes with fewer than 3 examples are removed. The texts are then embedded on the GPU; the dataset appears in this tab and in the training form, and with "run the full benchmark" also on the Overview. Up to 50 MB.

        text,label
"Die Lieferung kam zwei Wochen zu spät",shipping
"Wie ändere ich mein Passwort?",account
